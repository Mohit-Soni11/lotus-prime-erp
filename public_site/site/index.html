Backups in your control.

Keep a verified local backup, and optionally connect your own Google account to store a copy in the application's private area of your Google Drive.

  1. Choose a destination.

    Select a folder on your computer or external drive for local recovery copies.

  2. Connect Google when you choose.

    Sign in through Google's page and approve access to Lotus ERP's backup area and your basic account identity.

  3. Back up and verify.

    Create a manual backup or schedule daily, weekly or monthly backups while Lotus ERP is open and signed in.

This connection does not request Gmail-message access or access to unrelated Drive files. Available cloud storage depends on your Google account. Read the Privacy Policy for backup contents, storage and deletion information.
