Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Lotus ERP handles information for its Windows desktop application, Google account connection and backup/restore features. Google Drive backup is optional and uses the Google account you choose.

Information used by the application

Lotus ERP stores the business records entered by the shop's users on their computer. A backup may include business and local owner-account databases, invoices, customer and supplier records, inventory, payment and pledge records, shop settings, photos and documents. The shop owner controls the information entered and the backup destinations selected.

Google account connection

If you connect Google Drive, Google handles your sign-in and consent. Lotus ERP receives your basic account identity, including your email address and Google account identifier, and authorization tokens needed to access its private backup area. Your Google password is entered on Google's sign-in page, not in Lotus ERP.

The connection requests basic account identity and the Google Drive application-data permission. It does not request access to your Gmail messages or unrelated files in your Drive. You can use local backup without connecting a Google account.

How Google information is used

  • Your email address identifies the connected account in the application.
  • Authorization tokens allow the application to upload, list and download its backup files after you approve access.
  • Google Drive backup files are used for the backup and restore functions you enable.

Google information accessed by this integration is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Lotus ERP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where information is stored

Local business records and local backups are stored on your computer or the backup destination you select. Optional cloud backups are transmitted directly to your selected Google account's private Drive application-data area. This backup feature does not send the backup to a separate Lotus ERP server.

Google authorization tokens are stored using operating-system secure storage on the device. Google authorization tokens and active application login sessions are not exported in backup packages. Backup archives are not password-encrypted; protect the folders, devices and Google account where you store them.

Sharing and service providers

Google processes sign-in, account information and cloud backups under its own terms and Privacy Policy. Contacting application support shares the information you choose to include in your message with the support team and the email service involved. Do not send passwords, authorization tokens or complete business backups unless an appropriate support process has been agreed.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Local records and backups remain until you remove them or replace records through an authorized restore. Existing backups are not automatically deleted by the backup scheduler.

Selecting Disconnect removes the stored connection tokens from the device and attempts to revoke Google's authorization. If the device is offline, you can also revoke access through your Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not delete previously created backup files.

You can delete local backup files from the folders where you saved them. Google Drive provides controls for managing connected applications and their stored application data where available. Contact support if you need guidance locating the relevant Google controls. The application's developer does not receive direct access to backups stored in your Google account through this integration.

Restoring records

Restore is an explicit action that replaces current business records and settings with those in the selected backup. Lotus ERP checks the package and creates a local recovery backup before applying a restore. Review the date, contents and replacement notice before confirming.

This website

These information pages do not contain application sign-in forms, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the website. Following a Google or email link takes you to that provider's service.
